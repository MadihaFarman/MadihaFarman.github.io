About

I'm a final-year computer science student at KUST, and most of what I work on has to do with language. My final year project is an Urdu LLM that covers a broad range of tasks and can also reason. When I compared the existing Urdu models, none of them managed both, so that's the gap I'm working on.

The question came out of my internship at CallRollin this summer. Besides comparing three Urdu models, I tested about 60 Urdu text-to-speech outputs and found around 55 with mispronounced words. Unvocalized Urdu leaves out the diacritics that tell you which word is meant, so the system has to guess, and it often guesses wrong.

I also build smaller things to see how they work. I trained a GPT-style model on Shakespeare, wrote a chatbot that answers questions about KUST regulations in English, Urdu and Roman Urdu, and made a legal case system from 2,809 Supreme Court judgments. And I teach: I'm a teaching assistant for OOP at KUST this semester, and I've taught Python and machine learning to beginners and to practicing doctors.

Shakespeare text generation with a decoder-only Transformer

Oct 2026Code and notebook

A 10.8M-parameter GPT-style model (6 layers, 6 heads, 384-dimensional embeddings, 256-character context) written in PyTorch from the original Transformer and GPT-2 papers. I built it up from a bigram baseline and tested causal masking and scaled dot-product attention along the way. After 5,000 iterations on Tiny Shakespeare it reaches a train loss of 1.66 and a validation loss of 1.82.

  • Transformers

KUST AI Assistant, a bilingual RAG chatbot

Jun 2026Project link

Answers questions about official KUST documents (university statutes, the Universities Act, semester regulations) in English, Urdu script and Roman Urdu. Parent-child chunking keeps whole sections intact, and HyDE with query rewriting helps retrieval on short queries. Every answer cites its source document and page number. Voice input works in English and Urdu through Whisper, and the Next.js frontend handles right-to-left layout.

AI Legal Case Management System

Apr to May 2025Project link

Combines RAG-based precedent retrieval (HuggingFace embeddings and LLaMA-3.1, over 80% retrieval similarity) with two classifiers trained on 2,809 Supreme Court judgments. A voting ensemble predicts case category at 91.5% accuracy, and a stacking ensemble predicts priority at 95.2%. In testing, the Streamlit interface cut manual review effort by 70%.

  • RAG
  • Ensemble learning
  • Streamlit

  • Proposed a five-step plan for the team's own Urdu LLM: test base models (Qwen3, Llama-3.1, Gemma), build a billion-token Urdu corpus, fine-tune on human-written chain-of-thought data beyond math, set up an independent evaluation suite, and try RL-based reasoning tuning (GRPO).
