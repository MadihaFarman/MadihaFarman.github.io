Projects

Shakespeare text generation with a decoder-only Transformer

Oct 2026 · Code and notebook

A 10.8M-parameter GPT-style model (6 layers, 6 heads, 384-dimensional embeddings, 256-character context) written in PyTorch from the original Transformer and GPT-2 papers. I built it up from a bigram baseline, testing causal masking and scaled dot-product attention along the way. After 5,000 iterations on Tiny Shakespeare it reaches a train loss of 1.66 and a validation loss of 1.82.

  • PyTorch
  • Transformers
  • Language modeling

KUST AI Assistant: a bilingual RAG chatbot

Jun 2026 · Project link

Answers questions about official KUST documents (university statutes, the Universities Act, semester regulations) in English, Urdu script, and Roman Urdu. It uses parent-child chunking to keep whole sections intact, and HyDE with query rewriting to improve retrieval on short queries. Every answer cites its source document and page number. It also supports voice input in English and Urdu through Whisper, with a Next.js frontend that handles right-to-left layout.

  • HyDE

  • Next.js

AI Legal Case Management System

Apr to May 2025 · Project link

Combines RAG-based precedent retrieval (HuggingFace embeddings and LLaMA-3.1, over 80% retrieval similarity) with two classifiers trained on 2,809 Supreme Court judgments. A voting ensemble predicts case category at 91.5% accuracy, and a stacking ensemble predicts priority at 95.2%. A Streamlit interface cut manual review effort by 70% in testing.

  • Ensemble learning

Research experience

Research Intern, LLM and TTS

CallRollin, National Incubation Center, Islamabad · Jul to Sep 2026

  • Compared three Urdu LLMs (Alif-8B, Qalb-8B, Riazi-8B) on training data, fine-tuning method, and published results. None of them combines broad task coverage with strong reasoning.
  • Proposed a five-step plan for the team's own Urdu LLM: test base models (Qwen3, Llama-3.1, Gemma), build a billion-token Urdu corpus, fine-tune on human-written chain-of-thought data beyond math, set up an independent evaluation suite, and try RL-based reasoning tuning (GRPO).
  • Tested about 60 Urdu TTS outputs on unvocalized text and found about 55 mispronounced. I traced the errors to homograph ambiguity from missing diacritics (zair, zabar, pesh) and proposed a diacritization step before synthesis.
